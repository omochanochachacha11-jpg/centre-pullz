tipEn: >
  Side-to-side and front-to-back this box is as close to balanced as they
  come — never more than 2.5mm off centre either way — so you can push
  straight on and it will go straight. The height is the only variable: the
  figure drifts inside, so the weight can be 5mm below the middle or 1cm
  above it. Above the middle it wants to tip, below it wants to slide, so
  glance at where the figure is sitting and aim high or low to match. At
  300g in a small box it moves easily, so small accurate pushes beat
  forcing it. The Hiroshi Nohara figure from the same release uses the same
  box, if you want to compare.
