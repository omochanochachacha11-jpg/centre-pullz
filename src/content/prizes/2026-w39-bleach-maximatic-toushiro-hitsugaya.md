tipEn: >
  This is a good one to try if you're still getting the hang of it. The heavy
  part of the figure sits near the top of the box and right up against the back
  wall — and it barely moves around inside, so the box reacts the same way every
  time you touch it. That's the easy case. When the weight is up high, the box
  tips over instead of just sliding forward, and tipping is what gets it to
  fall. Go for the top back corner and push it away from you. At 398g it isn't
  heavy, so you don't need a big hit — a steady nudge does more than a hard one.
  Once you find a push that shifts it, just repeat exactly that.
