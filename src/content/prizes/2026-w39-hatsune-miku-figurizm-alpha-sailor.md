tipEn: >
  The weight is 5mm below the lid and 5mm from the back wall — about as high
  and as far back as it gets in a 27cm box. That is the easiest situation to
  work with, because a box this top-heavy tips over the moment its top edge
  passes the bridge; you don't need to lift anything. Push the top back edge
  straight away from you, near the middle since there's barely any side lean.
  The figure does move around inside, so check the box again before each push
  rather than assuming the weight stayed put.
