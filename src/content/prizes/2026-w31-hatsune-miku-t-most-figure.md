tipEn: >
  Front-back and top-bottom are both within 5mm of the middle, so the one
  number worth acting on is the side lean: a full 1.25cm to the right, which is
  a lot more than most boxes and makes the right-hand side clearly heavier.
  Push the right side and let the box's own imbalance help it go over that way.
  The shape works against you though — 505g in a box 23cm wide but only 10.5cm
  deep sits very solidly, so expect a firm push and only a little travel each
  time, and re-centre the claw if it starts to turn.
tipZh: >
